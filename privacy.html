Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

ClipStudio ("the App") runs on your own computer. We do not run servers that collect or store your data.

1. What data the App handles

  • Your videos and projects — stored only on your computer.
  • TikTok account data — when you connect TikTok, the App receives an access token and your basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) through TikTok Login Kit. This is used to show which account you are posting to and to post the videos you choose.

2. How the data is used

Only to provide the App's features: editing clips and, when you confirm, uploading or posting them to your TikTok account with the caption and privacy settings you chose. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Where it is stored

TikTok tokens and profile data are stored locally on your computer, in the App's settings folder. They are sent only to TikTok's official API. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

4. Third-party services

When you post, your video and caption are sent to TikTok and handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Your control

You can disconnect your TikTok account in the App at any time, which deletes the stored token from your computer. You can also revoke access in TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Uninstalling the App removes its local data.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue.
