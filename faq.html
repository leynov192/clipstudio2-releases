Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Which platforms can I download streams from?

Twitch videos and clips, YouTube videos, live recordings and Shorts, and any video file on your computer.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. ClipStudio can find moments without AI or with free AI on your own computer. A cloud AI key is optional and gives the best results.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, editing and rendering happen on your computer. Audio goes to a cloud speech service only if you choose cloud AI, and a clip goes to TikTok only when you post it.

How does posting to TikTok work?

You connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official sign-in. Before each post you see the video, edit the caption, choose who can view it and the interaction settings, and press Post. You can disconnect at any time.

Can I make clips of other streamers?

Only with the right to use the content — for example your own streams or streamers' clipping programs that allow it. You are responsible for the content you publish.

What format are the clips?

MP4, H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps, with stereo AAC sound — ready for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

How long does it take?

Rendering a clip takes 10–30 seconds on a modern PC. Finding moments in a 6-hour stream takes from a few minutes to about half an hour, depending on the mode.
