System requirements

Operating systemWindows 10 or 11 (64-bit); macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon
Processor and memory4 cores, 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended)
Video cardOptional. NVIDIA cards speed up rendering and speech recognition several times
Disk2 GB for the program, plus space for stream recordings (about 3–5 GB per hour of 1080p)
InternetNeeded to download streams and for cloud AI; editing and rendering work offline

Install

  1. Download the installer from the releases page.
  2. Run it — ClipStudio installs for your user and opens.
  3. Open Clips from a stream or Auto Clips and paste a stream link.
