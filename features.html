Features

Everything ClipStudio can do, section by section.

Clips from a stream

Load a recording from Twitch, YouTube or your disk, choose a range if you need only part of it, and let ClipStudio find the moments. Each moment gets a start and end time, a title and a short note on why it was picked. Watch any moment in the built-in player and move its borders.

Three ways to find moments

  • Cloud AI — the most accurate: speech recognition and moment choice by a cloud model with your own API key.
  • AI on your computer — free: speech by Whisper and moment choice by a local model, nothing leaves your PC.
  • Without AI — instant: the loudest reactions and the parts where the streamer talks the most.

Automatic framing

ClipStudio finds the streamer's face that stays in one place and the borders of the webcam around it, then places the game between the webcam and the overlay banners. Frames are remembered per channel, so the next stream of the same streamer is ready at once.

Editor

A timeline with video, text and layer tracks: trim and split, glue several moments into one clip, add images or videos on top, move and resize everything right on the frame. Undo, copy and paste, keyboard shortcuts.

Text and subtitles

Bold titles with an outline, a colour palette and colours for single words, several text layers, and automatic subtitles from the speech.

Style and banner

Two frame styles, an optional sponsor banner in the middle of the clip, a hook from the best seconds at the start, zooms on the face, subtle patterns and colour effects so every clip looks fresh.

Auto Clips

Paste a link, choose how many clips you need and press one button: download, framing, moment search and rendering run by themselves.

Share

Finished clips are 1080×1920 MP4 files with a light copy under 20 MB for messengers. Post to your own TikTok account from the program: you see the preview, edit the caption, pick who can view it and confirm — nothing is posted automatically.

Remote control

Control ClipStudio from your phone: a web remote in your home network and a Telegram bot to start projects and receive finished clips.
